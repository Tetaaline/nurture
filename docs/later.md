# Ideas for later

Things that came up while building the sprints and were deliberately not built, because the sprint did not ask for them.

- **Parent view of check-in trends, with teen consent.** The SRS keeps check-ins private; a future version could let a teen choose to share a weekly summary (counts only, no notes).
- **Reminders.** A gentle weekly nudge (email or push) when the family has not talked yet this week, opt-in.
- **Unlinking a family.** There is currently no self-service way to unlink; it needs a database change. A parent-initiated unlink with a confirm step would be reasonable.
- **Editing a display name** from the profile page.
- **Kinyarwanda and French translations** of the interface and the content.
- **Search across resources** once the content library grows.
- **Admin content editor** so the owner can publish and unpublish items without touching the seed file.
- **Account deletion from inside the app**, which should also delete journal entries and check-ins (the schema already cascades).
- **Streak history chart** showing counting weeks over the last few months.
- **Offline-friendly reading** of guides with a service worker.
